### Speaker Notes
\"If you look at the current competitive landscape, the industry is fractured.

On one side, you have the legacy giants—Ethereum and Solana. They have smart contracts and throughput, but their core cryptography—ECDSA and Ed25519—will be trivial for a quantum computer to break. They are building skyscrapers on a fault line.

On the other side, you have early quantum-resistant projects like QRL. They saw the threat early and adopted XMSS signatures. But they sacrifice everything else; they have no smart contract ecosystem, low throughput, and zero capability to handle modern decentralized finance.

Kontour Coin is the only architecture in the world that bridges this gap. We are the first protocol to combine the unbreakable quantum immunity of NIST-standard ML-DSA with the hyper-scalability of AI-driven routing and zero-knowledge rollups. We aren't just quantum-resistant; we are the fastest, smartest chain on the market. We make the legacy chains obsolete.\"
